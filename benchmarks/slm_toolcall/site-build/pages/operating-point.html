OPERATING POINT

Every arm at one false-positive budget

All {{fig:arms.scored}} arms are re-thresholded to the same block false-positive rate, {{fig:cap.exact}}, which is the incumbent cascade's own operating rate on this corpus. That budget allows {{fig:cap.maxfp}} false blocks out of {{fig:corpus.negatives}} benign cases. The threshold each arm needs to reach it differs by arm; the budget does not.

The reporting rule

Precision, recall, F1, accuracy and block FPR appear in one table, computed at one shared budget, over the {{fig:corpus.scorable}} scorable cases. No arm appears in that table at a threshold chosen for it alone. Each arm's own argmax is a separate table and is an in-sample upper bound.

The common operating point

At this budget the highest F1 among the {{fig:arms.candidates}} candidates is {{fig:cap.f1.arm}}: F1 {{fig:cap.f1}}, recall {{fig:cap.f1.recall}}, precision {{fig:cap.f1.precision}}. That is {{fig:cap.f1.tp}} true blocks of {{fig:corpus.positives}} positives and {{fig:cap.f1.fp}} false blocks of {{fig:corpus.negatives}} benign cases, at threshold {{fig:cap.f1.threshold}}. Each of the {{fig:arms.scored}} rows needed its own threshold to reach the budget, and {{fig:cap.thresholds}} distinct thresholds appear.

The highest recall any arm reaches inside this budget is {{fig:cap.f1.recall}}. The Wilson 95% interval on it runs to {{fig:res.wilson.hi}}. A filter at that recall leaves most destructive calls through, so no arm in this cohort is a guardrail on its own.

Recall at a zero-false-positive gate

The stricter gate admits no false block at all. {{fig:zfp.zero}} of the {{fig:zfp.candidates}} candidates retain zero recall under it. {{fig:zfp.nonzero}} retain some, and the highest is {{fig:zfp.best.arm}} at recall {{fig:zfp.best.recall}} over {{fig:zfp.best.tp}} true blocks.

Each arm at its own argmax

Every figure in this section is an in-sample oracle upper bound. The threshold is found by sweeping the same rows the score is computed on, so it is the ceiling an arm would reach if its threshold had been fitted with the labels in hand. It is not an operating point and it is not comparable with the common-budget table above, which is why the two are separate tables and never share a column.

The highest is {{fig:oracle.best.arm}} at {{fig:oracle.best.f1}} and the lowest is {{fig:oracle.min.arm}} at {{fig:oracle.min.f1}}. All {{fig:oracle.clear.floor}} arms clear the trivial floor of {{fig:floor.f1}} at their own argmax. The last column repeats each arm's F1 at the shared budget, so the size of the gap between a fitted ceiling and a fixed budget is visible per arm.

Shipped decisions against the trivial floor

Blocking every case scores block-only F1 {{fig:floor.f1}} at {{fig:corpus.prevalence}} prevalence. That row sits in the table below, at the bottom, so every F1 in the table is read against it. {{fig:arms.under.floor}} of the {{fig:arms.shipped.cands}} candidates with a shipped decision fall below it and {{fig:arms.zero.f1}} score exactly zero, blocking no positive case.

The untrained control-modernbert-base backbone scores {{fig:cb.shipped}} at its shipped decision and {{fig:cb.oracle}} at its own argmax. {{fig:beat.control.shipped}} of the candidates sit at or below it on shipped F1 and {{fig:beat.control.oracle}} at or below it on argmax F1. The decomposition of that result is on Results.

Accuracy, and the accuracy of doing nothing

Positive prevalence is {{fig:corpus.prevalence}} on s2 and {{fig:s3.prevalence}} on the second corpus, whose positives are {{fig:s3.gradeA.share}} grade A against s2's {{fig:s2.gradeA.share}}. At those prevalences accuracy is set by the benign class. Allowing every case scores {{fig:acc.s2.allow}} on s2 and {{fig:acc.s3.allow}} on the second corpus.

An arm's accuracy at the shared budget exceeds the all-allow figure exactly when it gains more true blocks than it spends on false ones. {{fig:acc.beat}} of the {{fig:arms.scored}} arms do, and the widest margin is {{fig:acc.gain}} cases out of {{fig:corpus.scorable}}. Both baselines sit in the same table as the accuracy figures.
