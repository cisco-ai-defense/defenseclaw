RANKING AND DIAGNOSTICS

How well each model orders cases, and why that is not the answer

F1 at the budget, on the leaderboard, answers the deployment question. This page is diagnostic: AUC with no threshold, what length alone predicts, what an untrained backbone scores, and how far each model's default decision sits from a usable one.

AUC, with and without length control

AUC is the chance a model scores a random destructive call above a random benign one. For {{fig:corpus.positives}} positives and {{fig:corpus.negatives}} benign cases the Hanley–McNeil standard error at AUC 0.5 is {{fig:band.se}}, so the 95% chance band is [{{fig:band.lo}}, {{fig:band.hi}}]. On raw AUC {{fig:auc.above}} of the {{fig:models.scored}} models sit above that band and {{fig:auc.below}} below it. Every model is scored on one fixed variable chosen by its class structure, and each is a single monotone scalar, so AUC definitions A and B coincide for all of them.

Raw AUC overstates what a model knows. Destructive trajectories in this corpus are longer than benign ones, and {{fig:cue.n}} counting variables with no model in them separate the two: {{fig:cue.list}}. Length-controlled AUC removes that: the AUC inside each prompt-length quintile, pooled with each quintile weighted by the positive-benign pairs it holds.

The leakage gate

Both ModernBERT models are bare ModernBertForMaskedLM backbones with no trained head, read by a zero-shot masked-token yes/no probe. The prediction made before measuring was that both land at chance once prompt length is controlled for. Under the published estimator that prediction {{fig:ctrl.verdict}}.

Calibration

Each model's block score is bucketed into {{fig:cal.buckets}} equal-width bins over the {{fig:corpus.scorable}} scorable cases, and each bin's observed positive rate is plotted against its mean predicted probability. {{fig:cal.sparse}} of the filled bins hold fewer than 20 cases, so each point's case count is drawn with it. A model far from the diagonal is miscalibrated at its default decision even if it orders cases well.

deberta-v3-prompt-injection-v2 and its 512-token window

The AUC leader is a {{fig:deb.params.short}}-parameter encoder whose window is min(token_budget, max_position_embeddings − 2) = {{fig:trunc.cap}} tokens. {{fig:trunc.cases}} of {{fig:corpus.scorable}} cases ({{fig:trunc.casespct}}) have at least one truncated event, and {{fig:trunc.allcases}} have every event truncated. Truncation does not track its errors: AUC is {{fig:trunc.auc}} on truncated cases and {{fig:trunc.aucun}} on the rest. At its default decision it blocks {{fig:deb.fp}} of {{fig:corpus.negatives}} benign cases, more than either control ({{fig:deb.fp.cb}} and {{fig:deb.fp.cl}}); at the budget it catches {{fig:cap.deberta.tp}} positive.
