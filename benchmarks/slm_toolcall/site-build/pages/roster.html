Gating

{{fig:roster.gated}} of {{fig:roster.n}} arms need a licence-accepted HuggingFace token, across {{fig:roster.groups}} separate acceptance groups: the Gemma family, Llama 3.2, and a third group covering Llama Prompt Guard 2 at licence other. All three were accepted on {{fig:gate.cleared}}. A 403 is the signal that the token is valid and that repo's group is unaccepted; a 401 means the token itself is not. {{fig:roster.ungated}} arms fetch with no acceptance.

Anyone repeating the cohort has to accept three separate licences before {{fig:roster.gated}} of the arms will fetch. Community re-uploads of the gated weights were refused: a mirror launders the provenance the licence column records.

Encoder backbones

The encoder arm of the cohort is {{fig:roster.encoder}} trained classifiers against {{fig:roster.control}} untrained MLM controls. Both Prompt Guard 2 sizes are DebertaV2ForSequenceClassification with default LABEL_0 / LABEL_1 heads, so all three trained encoders share the DeBERTa-v2 backbone family. That is three checkpoints inside one family.

An earlier framing held that Prompt Guard 2 supplied an encoder backbone independent of DeBERTa. That was wrong and is withdrawn. The only independent encoder backbone in the cohort is ModernBERT, and both ModernBERT arms are controls. A trained-encoder result from this cohort can be shown to be non-checkpoint-specific inside the DeBERTa-v2 family; it cannot be separated from a DeBERTa-family result.

The evidence is in the download record. The weight manifest for both Prompt Guard 2 sizes records architectures as {{fig:pg2.arch}} and max_position_embeddings as {{fig:pg2.maxpos}}, which is the same backbone family and the same window as the DeBERTa candidate.

Prompt Guard 2's repo names understate its size. The headline 22M is {{fig:pg2.22}} parameters and the headline 86M is {{fig:pg2.86}}, because the published figures exclude embeddings.

Qwen3

Qwen3 at 0.6B, 1.7B and 4B was dropped on a non-China provenance constraint. The download record for those {{fig:qwen.n}} repositories totals {{fig:qwen.bytes}} bytes, {{fig:qwen.gib}} GiB, and no GPU time was spent. They were the strongest ungated general models at their sizes.

With Gemma 3 and Llama 3.2 both gated, the ungated non-China general field in this cohort contains nothing that is simultaneously best-in-class for its size and permissively licensed.
