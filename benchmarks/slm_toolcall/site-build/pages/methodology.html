Reporting rules

  • One budget. Precision, recall, F1 and block FPR are reported at block FPR {{fig:cap.exact}}, the existing cascade guardrail's own rate on s2. Each model's threshold is the one with the highest recall that holds it, ties broken toward the tighter threshold.
  • Scorable cases only. Every F1, AUC, FPR and confusion count is over the {{fig:corpus.scorable}} cases of grade A, B or D. The {{fig:corpus.gradeC}} grade-C cases are kept for diagnostics and counted nowhere.
  • Oracle figures are labelled. A best F1 found by sweeping thresholds on the rows it is scored on is an oracle upper bound. It is shown only in its own column, never as a result.
  • Accuracy only beside all-allow accuracy ({{fig:acc.s2.allow}} on s2), because at {{fig:corpus.prevalence}} prevalence the benign class sets it.
  • No table over eight columns; the four confusion counts share one tp/fp/fn/tn cell.
  • One ranking variable per class structure. P(block) for three-class models and block.yes for the two-head model; the single positive-class probability for two-class models. P(block) − P(confirm) is never ranked on: on the System One programme it inverted below chance on a disjoint corpus. For the {{fig:models.twoclass}} two-class models ({{fig:models.twoclass.controls}} of them controls) P(confirm) does not exist, so neither sum nor difference is computed.
  • Neither gate is durable. When the System One programme moved thresholds to a disjoint corpus, 4 of 24 zero-false-positive gates survived, 3 of them degenerate, and false-positive caps held in 8 of 48.

Intervals

Recall and block FPR carry Wilson 95% intervals at z = {{fig:wilson.z}}. F1 carries a percentile bootstrap over cases, {{fig:boot.n}} resamples at seed {{fig:boot.seed}}, with each model's threshold held fixed, so it covers sampling of the corpus but not re-fitting the threshold. The zero-false-positive gate carries the rule-of-three bound 3/{{fig:corpus.negatives}}. The best pair of models is the best of {{fig:over.pairs}} evaluated on the rows it is scored on, so it is an in-sample selection.

Length control, and why this estimator

The corpus is cut into five quintiles of natural prompt tokens, the same bins for every model. AUC is computed inside each and pooled with each bin weighted by its positive-benign pairs: the {{fig:est.published}} estimator, named in {{fig:est.source}}.

  • What it estimates. Pooling is the Mann-Whitney statistic over within-quintile pairs: raw AUC with the length-confounded pairs removed.
  • Weight against evidence. The thinnest quintile holds {{fig:est.q0.pos}} of {{fig:corpus.positives}} positives, {{fig:est.q0.share}} of the evidence. An unweighted mean gives it {{fig:est.q0.wunw}} of the weight, an over-weighting of {{fig:est.overweight}}×; pair weighting gives it {{fig:est.q0.wpool}}.
  • Variance. Per-bin Hanley–McNeil at AUC 0.5 puts the unweighted mean's standard error at {{fig:est.seratio}}× the pooled estimator's; it is worse on every candidate ({{fig:est.seworse}} exceptions).

The ranking artifact evaluates {{fig:est.schemes}} schemes, and {{fig:est.unwfam}} of them are unweighted means that differ only in binning, so a tally across them is not evidence. The table gives every model under four of them.

When a run counts

A run counts only if its metadata says complete: true and the on-disk sha256 of its predictions equals the recorded prediction_sha256. The runner harness/score_arm.py writes neither; harness/settle.py adds both after requiring {{fig:settle.rows}} untorn rows, then re-reads and re-hashes to confirm. The digest is taken over the complete-line prefix at that point, so it certifies the body unchanged since then, not as the runner wrote it. {{fig:settle.settled}} of the {{fig:models.scored}} models are complete, hash-verified runs, with {{fig:settle.mismatch}} digest mismatches. {{fig:settle.unsettled.note}}

Scorer equivalence

The harness imports the reference driver's build_state and build_questions, so prompts are byte-exact. Before any model here is reported, the pipeline re-derives {{fig:parity.n}} published System One scorecards and must match them. Those reference models are published on the System One Space, not here. All {{fig:arith.importers}} scoring scripts import their arithmetic verbatim from {{fig:arith.path}}, {{fig:arith.bytes}} bytes at sha256 {{fig:arith.sha}}.

What is published

Aggregates only. No per-case row detail appears on this Space; every figure is a count, rate or distribution over the corpus or a stratum of it. mcptox is local-evaluation-only and one further source is aggregate-only; a census of the source.dataset field found 0 rows from either in either corpus. Licences and redistribution markers for every source are in {{fig:lock.path}}, {{fig:lock.entries}} entries at sha256 {{fig:lock.sha}}.

The four data repositories that hold the corpora and the prediction files stay private. This Space is public and carries no corpus rows, no provider rationales, no credentials and no unredacted identifiers. Corpus cases_sha256 {{fig:corpus.sha}}.

Build gates

  • site-build/build.py recomputes {{fig:gate.asserts}} figures from their primitives and checks each against its artifact before any page is written, over {{fig:gate.artifacts}} input files and {{fig:gate.charts}} charts. It aborts on any mismatch.
  • Each ROC curve is carried as integer (false blocks, true blocks) pairs, {{fig:curve.points}} points across {{fig:curve.models}} models. The area under each drawn curve must equal the published raw AUC exactly, on all {{fig:curve.aucs}} AUCs, and the budget point must lie on the curve.
  • site-build/guard.py, byte-identical to the System One Space's copy, fails on any corpus case id, sensitive-text shingle, CJK codepoint or credential shape, and on any file that is not .html .css .md .json .txt or is over 1 MiB.
  • site-build/verify.py checks structure, links, SVG bounds, table width, the reading precision of every figure, the required disclosures and the list of retired figures.
  • site-build/publish.py re-runs both, records the repository's visibility, uploads, and aborts if the visibility changed. It never moves a repository between public and private.
