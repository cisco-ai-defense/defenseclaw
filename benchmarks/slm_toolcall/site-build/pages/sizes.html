The cohort split by counted parameters

Three bands: under 3B, 3B to 6B, and 6B and up. The cohort holds {{fig:band.under}} arms in the first, {{fig:band.mid}} in the second and {{fig:band.upper}} in the third. The smallest arm is {{fig:band.min.arm}} at {{fig:band.min.params}} parameters and the largest is {{fig:band.max.arm}} at {{fig:band.max.params}}, a spread of {{fig:band.spread}}×.

How the bands are drawn

The cut points are 3×109 and 6×109 counted parameters. Each arm's count comes from its own run metadata and is checked against the arm registry at build time; the build fails if the two disagree. Every arm inside a band is reported at the one common false-positive budget of {{fig:cap.exact}}, so the columns compare across bands as well as within them.

Size and the operating point

The best F1 at the common budget is {{fig:cap.f1}}, held by {{fig:cap.f1.arm}} at {{fig:cap.f1.params}} counted parameters, which places it in the under-3B band. Of the {{fig:arms.candidates}} candidates, {{fig:zfp.zero}} retain zero recall at a zero-false-positive gate, and that set spans both populated bands.

The binding constraint measured on this cohort is the grade composition of the positives, which is a property of the corpora and is set out on Datasets. The grade A share of positives is {{fig:s2.gradeA.share}} on s2 against {{fig:s3.gradeA.share}} on the second corpus.

Parameter counts are read from each arm's run metadata inside cohort-length-controlled-ranking.json and cross-checked against harness/arms.py. Operating-point figures are at block FPR ≤ {{fig:cap.exact}} over cases_sha256 {{fig:corpus.sha}}.
