Security Properties:
-------------------
- Content scan runs before capability checks (defense in depth)
- SSRF blocks private IPs before policy lookup (fail-secure)
- Synchronous blocks (no speculative execution for content/SSRF)
- Audit trail with DCLAW_REASON_CONTENT_BLOCK and DCLAW_REASON_SSRF_BLOCK

Key Design Decisions:
--------------------
1. Content scan at stage 3 (early, after rate limit, before hash deny)
2. SSRF at stage 5 (before policy table, with network cap guard)
3. Trust boundary inference uses direction (REQUEST=USER_INPUT, RESPONSE=TOOL_OUTPUT)
4. Backward compat: NULL/zero-length content skips scan
5. No malloc: scan_ctx on stack, findings array bounded at compile-time
